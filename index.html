OUR PURPOSE

About LifeLink

LifeLink is a student project designed to make it easier for people to find potential blood donors during emergencies. It brings together donor search, donor registration and emergency blood requests in one simple platform.

Our aim is to spread awareness about blood donation and encourage people to help others in need.
