About LifeLink

LifeLink is a student project designed to make it easier for people to find potential blood donors during emergencies.

The platform provides options to search for donors, register as a donor and submit emergency blood requests through a simple and user-friendly design.

Our aim is to spread awareness about blood donation and encourage people to help others in need.
